Our Culinary Story

Where Tradition Meets Modern Sophistication

Nestled in Buldhana, The Urban Plate was founded on a simple yet profound vision: to elevate everyday dining into an unforgettable sensory journey. We celebrate Maharashtra's proud culinary traditions infused with contemporary gastronomic techniques.

Whether you are dining with family, hosting an intimate anniversary celebration, or catching up over artisan coolers and clay-pot biryanis, our team welcomes you with warmth and attention to every culinary detail.

Farm-to-Fork Freshness

We source local vegetables and cold-pressed oils from trusted growers across the Vidarbha region.

Craft & Perfection

Authentic tandoori marinades, slow-simmered brass handi gravies, and zero artificial coloring.

"Cooking is not just about recipes; it's about connecting memories, aromas, and genuine laughter across the dinner table."
— Executive Chef Vikram Deshmukh (Sample Highlight)
